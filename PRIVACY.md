# Privacy policy

Effective: 3 October 2026

ChatGPT & Claude Exporter – PDF & Markdown processes the conversation or webpage you choose to export locally in your browser. It does not send that content to the extension developer or an export server.

The extension reads the current conversation from the AI provider using your existing signed-in browser session, or from page content when direct data is unavailable. Those requests go only to the provider you are already using. Authentication values remain in the page context and are not included in exports.

No analytics, advertising, tracking, account registration or developer-operated data storage is used. Conversation and page content are held temporarily in memory. PDF blob URLs are temporary. Downloaded files remain on your device until you remove them. Copying a webpage writes its Markdown to your system clipboard; other software with clipboard access may read it.

Access to supported AI sites allows the Export interface and conversation extraction. Other webpages are accessed only when you invoke the right-click copy command. The extension does not crawl your browsing history or automatically copy pages.

Your AI provider, browser and operating system have their own privacy practices. This policy covers only this extension.

For privacy issues, contact the developer through the support contact on the Chrome Web Store listing.
